A ride to the Bangalore Airport

This is not for the weak hearted...

It's 3pm the day before you leave for your flight the next day. Your flight departs at 6am tomorrow. You stay 38km away from the airport, in Jayanagar in Bangalore.

You need to plan to get to the airport. Even before you start your trip, there are a few things that you might want know, based on the facts stated above

Anxiety 1: You are not sure if you'll get enough sleep before leaving for the airport. Actually you are more concerned about whether your cab will arrive on time to pick you up, or will they cancel last moment or whether they'll cancel after you hail the cab and the driver asks you to cancel trip on the app and pay direct cash to him.

But somehow you book an Uber the night before scheduled at 03:20 AM, hoping the cab would arrive by 03:30 AM expecting to take 1 hour 30 mins to reach the airport at 05:00, leaving you an hour to finish security and check-in at the airport.

\

You set an alarm for 02:40 am + 5 min snooze, leaving 45 mins to freshen up and be ready for your 3 hour Indigo flight.

It's 3pm the day before you leave for your flight the next day. Your flight departs at 6am tomorrow. You stay 38km away from the airport, in Jayanagar in Bangalore.

You need to plan to get to the airport. Even before you start your trip, there are a few things that you might want know, based on the facts stated above

You somehow wake up after sleeping at 11:30 trying to look for the most reasonable cab options, but somehow how settle on booking an Uber and ready to pay 💰1300 for a one-way ride towards the end-of-the-earth airport, because it's a little more reliable than Ola or Deepam cabs.

But you wake up half-asleep to realise that this 'schedule' option doesn't confirm the cab booking - it just starts looking for a cab at the scheduled time - how transprent!

Because of this Uber apps, new feature + few bug fixes - your bowel movements speed up and you are actually fresh and ready by 03:05

a little Anxious face

You cancel the scheduled time and start searching for an UberGo immediately - this time for 1500

Alternatively, you also consider walking to the Jayanagar bus stop to hail the KIA-5, but 'what-if-I-have-wait' makes you sweat.

While the uber cab request is on, you try looking for live KIA-5 bus time that arrives at the nearest bus stop. But alas, BMTC is more secretive than CIA.

You luckily get a cab. Ramalingam is arriving in 8 minutes.

You do last minute bag checks, make sure you are not carrying any battery packs or weapons in your hand bag. But this takes 11 mins.

You wait for Ramalingam.

You call him using the goddamn paid call option - to get on his phone immediately

Ramalingam's phone is busy. You wonder if he is busy distance calling his tinder match at 03:40 AM. You call him back - this time when he picks up, you beg him not to 'cancel' the ride and just arrive at the pick-up point.

Ramalingam calls you back at 03:42 AM, telling he's arrived at the pick-up location, but somehow you don't spot him, while you are waiting in the middle of the road. You figure out that he's on the parallel road - so you ask him to come to the next one at the 'actual location', while you give directions over the phone, in your half-baked Kannada

Ramalingam finally arrives at 03:52 and you give him your suitcase

Ramalingam opens the trunk but can't fit your suitcase, because there's a CNG cylinder in his trunk, so he adjusts the seat and puts in the passenger seat, being fussy that it will dirty his car seat, but the car smelt like wet socks/tip-top

It's 04:07 AM. You start feeling anxious

But, before you give him his OTP for the ride, he asks how much is the fare on the uber app?

You start to think 'here we go'. You tell him you are not cancelling the ride to him cash. He somehow agrees, starts the ride

...

You leave at 04:11. You check google maps to Kempegowda ISS airport. It shows your ETA to be 05:10 - just enough time to reach Indigo's counter

You tell Ramalingam to hurry.

To keep yourself calm and distracted, you plug in your ear phones and listen to calming-sleep-sounds playlist on Spotify

After some-time, you notice Ramalingam is pulling down towards a petrol pump. You ask him what's the matter

He says, he needs to refuel. You remind him that his car is CNG - but he corrects you that it's hybrid and currently running on diesel.

He assures you this will just take 2 mins. just 2 mins.

You get slightly tensed, so you switch to even-more-calming-sleep-sounds playlist.

He refuels and asks you for breaking a 500 rupee note. You say you don't have it. You hear him talking to the fuel station attendent in Kannada - they somewhat settle it and he gets back on the driver seat

You leave after 8 mins. Your ETA now is 05:27

You ask him to ride faster, to hurry while you tighten your seat-belt and assume position to puke outside of the car window - just in case

You see the Bangalore toll gate is front of you. As you are about to arrive there, Ramalingam takes a u-turn

You panick and almost shout and ask what's he doing. He says there's a faster route without toll. You have no choice at this point in time really.

You stop playing calming music and swollow your tongue.

You somehow reach the terminal (alive and with dry pants). You hurry with your luggage to the gate

The security asks you for your Digi-Yatra. You tell them you don't have Digi-Yatri, so they ask you to join a longer queue

At this time you request fellow queue-members to let you through because of a hurry. They are nice and let you. You make it through and join another queue at the Indigo check-in counter while you hear their staff yelling "Any passengers to Delhi"?

You hear your guardian angel and you go to the priority check-in

The END

The story is an amalgamation of 60 rides to/from the airport and after spending about 36,000 to/from airports alone. Here is the entire data analysis for it on Observable. One could have at least 19 heart attacks.

Just so that this never happens to you, here's information that will help you.

  • Bengawalk's BMTC KIA bus route. Bus staff are very nice, polite and also accept G-pay
  • App based cabs vs Train vs Buses vs Taxis
