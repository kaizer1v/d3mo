# Data Story Template

Rules & Constraints
1. Needs to be out in 1 week (7 days)
2. Use whatever tools possible, whatever medium possible 

## The interest / Rational / Topic
pick anytopic and write why you picked it here

## EDA
look at the data and do some basic analysis to figure whats in it. what seems interesting.

## Who is it for
write about who might be reading this? what would they expect/want to see?

## Skeleton
write the skeleton of the story, the highs, the lows, the expectation from this piece

## Visual language
define the communcation channel, style and language to communicate the data and the story

## Compose
put it together using any goddamn tool

## Test / Share
get some feedback, show it to people, share it on channels & take note of it
